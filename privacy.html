Sudoku Hint

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Sudoku Hint.

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Overview

Sudoku Hint is an advertisement-free app. It does not require you to create a user account and it is not designed to collect your name, address, contacts or precise location.

Sudoku photographs

When you choose to analyse a Sudoku, the photograph is sent securely to the app’s backend and an AI service provider so the grid and handwritten entries can be interpreted and a hint can be generated. The photograph is used to provide the requested analysis. You should avoid including people, documents or other personal information in the photograph.

Technical and usage information

The service may process limited technical information needed to operate and protect the app, such as a pseudonymous app or customer identifier, request timestamps, error information, hint balance and basic server logs. This information is used for functionality, fraud prevention, troubleshooting and service reliability.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Purchase entitlement and hint-balance information may also be processed by the app’s purchase-management provider. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

Advertising and tracking

Sudoku Hint contains no third-party advertising and does not sell personal information. The app does not use information for cross-app advertising tracking.

Service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that supply hosting, AI image analysis, app distribution and in-app purchase functionality. They process information to provide their respective services and are subject to their own legal and security obligations.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain hint balances, investigate errors, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Server logs and provider records may be retained for limited operational periods.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information during transmission and processing. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Sudoku Hint is a general puzzle utility and is not specifically directed at children. The app does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Your choices

You control whether a photograph is taken and submitted. Camera access can be removed in the iPhone’s Settings. You may contact the developer with privacy questions or requests concerning information associated with the app.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.
